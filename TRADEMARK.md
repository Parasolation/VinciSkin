# Trademark

The GPL covers VinciSkin's code. It does not cover the project's name, logo or icon, and this
file says what that means for a build you hand to somebody else.

## What the GPL already lets you do

Use, study, modify and redistribute the code, including for money, as long as you follow the
GPL. Nothing here narrows that.

## What this file asks of a distributed build

- **Rename it.** Choose your own name and replace the project's logo, icon and the marks under
  `src/assets/icons/` that identify it. A fork you keep to yourself can keep the name. The
  moment you distribute it, it needs its own.
- **Do not present it as official.** Say it is based on VinciSkin and link back to this
  repository, rather than suggesting the project endorses or supports your build.
- **Do not sign it as the project.** The project's code signing certificate is not yours to
  use. Sign your build with your own certificate, or leave it unsigned.
- **Do not use the name or logo to suggest sponsorship or affiliation** with League Toolkit or
  the VinciSkin project.

## Patcher binaries

`ltk_patcher_dll.dll` and `ltk_patcher_host.exe` carry their own terms in
[LTK-PATCHER-LICENSE.md](LTK-PATCHER-LICENSE.md), including the rule about signatures. Where the
two files disagree about a binary, the patcher license wins.
